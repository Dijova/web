About Clau Cleaning Corp

Clean you can see. Safety you can trust.

Clau Cleaning Corp is a commercial cleaning company based in Dracut, Massachusetts. We specialize in the disinfection and maintenance of medical facilities — clinics and doctor's offices — and public and private schools. We also offer house cleaning, carpet cleaning and floor care.

Our team follows the technical protocols needed to meet strict hygiene standards: color-coded tools, hospital-grade disinfectants and a focus on high-touch, critical surfaces to stop cross-contamination before it starts.

  • Trained, uniformed and background-checked staff
  • EPA-registered, hospital-grade disinfectants
  • Custom cleaning plans and institutional contracts
  • Quality inspections and a single point of contact

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you clean medical clinics and doctor's offices?

Yes. Medical facilities are our specialty. We clean and disinfect exam rooms, waiting areas, restrooms and offices using hospital-grade, EPA-registered disinfectants and protocols designed to prevent cross-contamination.

Do you work with public and private schools?

Yes. We provide daily and periodic cleaning for public and private schools, scheduled after hours or on weekends so classes are never interrupted.

What areas of Massachusetts do you serve?

We are based in Dracut, MA and serve Lowell, Methuen, Lawrence, Tewksbury, Chelmsford, Andover, Billerica, Tyngsborough and the rest of the Merrimack Valley.

How do I get a quote?

Fill out the form below or call +1 978-328-2406. We schedule a free walkthrough of your facility and send a written quote, usually within 48 hours.

Do you also offer house cleaning and carpet cleaning?

Yes. Besides commercial work, we clean homes on a weekly, bi-weekly or monthly basis, handle move-in and move-out cleanings, and provide carpet cleaning and floor care.

Do you offer long-term maintenance contracts?

Yes. Most of our clients are on recurring daily, weekly or monthly plans with a dedicated crew and scheduled quality inspections.
