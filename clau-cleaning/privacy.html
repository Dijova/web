Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Clau Cleaning Corp ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through claucleaningcorp.com and how we protect it.

Information we collect

  • Data you enter in the quote form: name, email, phone, service requested and message.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends automatically (such as page visited).

How we use it

Only to answer your request, prepare a quote and provide our cleaning services. We never sell or rent your information.

How we protect it

  • Data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Requests are stored in a private, access-restricted account protected with 2-step verification.
  • We filter spam and malicious content, and we will never ask you for passwords, bank details or payments by email or through this form.

Retention and your rights

We keep requests only as long as needed to serve you. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing claucleaningcorpc2@gmail.com or calling +1 978-328-2406.

Third parties

The site loads photos from Pexels and a Google Maps embed. Links to Facebook and Instagram open those platforms, which have their own privacy policies.
